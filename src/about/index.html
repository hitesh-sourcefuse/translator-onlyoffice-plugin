Cell Translator

Translates spreadsheet cells in place and keeps a record of every translation inside the workbook itself.

The record lives in a hidden _CellTranslator sheet, so it travels with the file when it is copied, emailed or renamed. Repeated text is translated once and reused, and any translated cell can be reverted to its original.

Three engines are available:

  • Bergamot — runs locally via WebAssembly. No server, works offline. Language models are downloaded once and cached.
  • LibreTranslate — a self-hosted server. The only engine that can suggest alternative translations.
  • AI — any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Also used to refine a single cell with "Improve with AI".
